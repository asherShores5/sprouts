How to Play Sprouts

  1. Players take turns drawing a curve between any two live spots (or from a spot back to itself).
  2. The curve must not cross any existing curve or pass through any existing spot.
  3. After drawing, a new spot is placed at the midpoint of the curve.
  4. Each spot can have at most 3 connections. A spot with 3 connections is dead and cannot be used.
  5. The player who makes the last valid move wins.

💡 A spot used as both endpoints of a loop counts as 2 connections. The new midpoint spot starts with 2 connections.
